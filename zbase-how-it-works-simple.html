zBase — explained simply

Think of it as a coat check for crypto payments.

Your AI bot pays for stuff online. Right now, every payment is public — like writing your name and address on a billboard every time you buy coffee. zBase fixes that. Without breaking any rules.

The problem, in normal words

💵

How cash works

You hand someone a $5 bill for coffee. Nobody knows where you got that bill, what else you bought today, or what you'll buy next. That's normal.

📡

How crypto payments work today

Every payment your AI agent makes is broadcast forever. Anyone can see:

  • Which AI services you use (Claude? GPT? Gemini?)
  • How much you spent and when
  • Your complete history — going back forever
  • Your competitors can copy your strategy in real time.

The simple analogy

zBase is a coat check at a party. You drop your coat off. So does everyone else. Later, you grab "a coat" — and nobody can tell whose was whose.

Except instead of coats, it's USDC. And the magic ticket that proves you have one is math, not a paper stub.

What actually happens, step by step

1
Public · one time

Your bot deposits some USDC into the pool

Anyone can see you put money in — that's fine. Hundreds of others do too. You're now part of the crowd.

Why public: you need to prove later that you really did deposit. So this part is recorded openly, just like cash going into a coat check.
2
Off-chain

Your bot tries to use a paid service

It calls Anthropic (or any service that accepts x402). The service says: "Cool, pay me $1 first."

Why this matters: x402 is the new "pay-per-API-call" standard. Bots already use it. zBase plugs into it — no rewrites needed.
3
The magic step

zBase makes a math proof: "I have a deposit"

Takes about 2.5 seconds. The proof is real (Anthropic can verify it), but it doesn't reveal which deposit. It's the wristband, not your ID.

Why this works: zero-knowledge proofs are 30-year-old math. Same tech used in Vitalik Buterin's privacy research. We didn't invent it — we packaged it.
4
Private settlement

The pool pays Anthropic. Your bot's name is nowhere.

On the public ledger it reads "the pool paid Anthropic $1." Not "your bot paid." The link is broken.

The result: wallet trackers see the pool moving. They can't see you. Your strategy stays yours.
5
Bonus

While your money sits, it earns interest

Idle deposits get lent out automatically (to Kamino on Solana, Morpho on Base). You earn around 4–9% APY — for free, while you wait.

Why nobody else does this: we wrote the contract to handle it. Other privacy tools just let money sit. We make it work for you.

Questions you probably have

Is this like Tornado Cash? Will I get sanctioned?

No. Tornado Cash had zero compliance — it let dirty money mix with clean money. zBase only accepts deposits from approved addresses (called an "ASP"). It's the design Vitalik Buterin co-authored specifically because Tornado got sanctioned. The US Treasury (March 2026) explicitly acknowledged that mixers have "legitimate privacy uses" when they're compliant. zBase is.

Can my bot get its money back? What if I lose the secret?

Yes — you withdraw the same way you pay, using your secret code. The secret stays in your local app (encrypted). Lose it and the money is stuck in the pool forever, same as losing your private key. Back it up.

How do I plug it into my existing AI agent?

Change one line. If your agent already speaks x402 (Coinbase's payment standard for bots), point its facilitator URL at zBase instead of Coinbase. That's it. No code rewrite, no SDK swap.

What if the zBase server goes down?

The pool lives on-chain (Solana + Base). Anyone can run a facilitator — the code is open source (Apache 2.0). If our server dies, you spin up your own, or use a community one. Your money is not on our server. Ever.
